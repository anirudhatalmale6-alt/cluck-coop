How the chicken farm works

1 Get Started

Create your account with a username and password. If a friend gave you a referral code, enter it when you sign up — you'll both get a coin bonus.

You start with an empty farm and no chicken. Head to the 🐣 Chickens tab and grab your first one — buying is free right now.

2 The Chickens

Each chicken lays one egg every 3 hours. Better chickens lay eggs worth more coins.

💡 Buying is free for now, so you can try every chicken. Coin prices will apply later.

3 Claim Your Eggs

After a chicken finishes its 3 hours, a 🧺 Claim button appears on it. Tap it to collect the egg into your basket.

The timer keeps running even when the app or browser is closed — come back later and your eggs will be waiting (up to 8 saved per chicken).

In a hurry? The 🧺 Collect Eggs button at the top claims every ready chicken at once.

4 Sell for Coins

Open the 🏪 Market and sell your eggs for coins. Use those coins to buy more chickens, expand your farm, and grow your flock.

5 Invite Friends & Earn Free Chickens

Every player gets their own referral code in the 👤 Profile tab. Share it!

  • When a friend signs up with your code, you both get bonus coins.
  • For every 10 friends who join with your code and buy a chicken, you earn 1 free chicken 🐔 automatically.

Your Profile tab tracks how many friends joined, how close you are to your next free chicken, and how many you've already won.
